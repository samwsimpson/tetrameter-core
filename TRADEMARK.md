# Trademark policy

The code in this repository is licensed under Apache 2.0. **The name is not.**

Apache 2.0 is explicit about this in section 6: the licence grants copyright and patent rights, and
grants no trademark rights at all. That is a normal arrangement for open-source projects with a
commercial sponsor, and it is worth stating plainly rather than leaving people to discover it.

"Tetrameter" and the four-tick mark are trademarks of KumoKodo.

## What you may do, freely

- Use, modify, distribute and sell software built with this library, commercially or not.
- Fork it, publish your fork, and change whatever you like.
- Say your product "uses Tetrameter", "is built on Tetrameter", or "is compatible with Tetrameter".
  Accurate, factual references to the project by name are fine and always will be.
- Reproduce the name in documentation, articles, talks and comparisons, including critical ones.

## What needs our permission

- Naming your fork or product "Tetrameter", or a name close enough to be confused with it.
- Using the mark in a way that suggests we produced, endorsed or support your product.
- Using it in a domain name, an npm package name, a company name or a logo.

## Why we care about this one specifically

This library exists to make carbon and cost figures checkable. Its whole value is that when a number
carries our name, you can trace where it came from, which factor version produced it, and whether
that factor has since been restated.

If a fork with stale coefficients published figures under the same name, the damage would not be to
our brand. It would be to somebody's disclosure. Emission factors go out of date silently and keep
producing confident, wrong answers, which is exactly why the maintained version has a restatement
log and a version on every factor.

Keeping the name attached to the maintained version is how a reader knows which one they are looking
at.
